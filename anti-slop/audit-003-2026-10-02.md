# antislop audit 003: interface wording

Date: 2026-10-02
Mode: AFTER (audit first; nothing is changed until specific numbers are approved)
Scope: every sentence shown on screen in `frontend/src` (pages, components, help, printed sheets), read in place.
Method: all on-screen text of sentence length extracted and read; rules from the antislop core and the copywriting skill. No rewrite adds a fact that is not already in the app.

Priority follows the rule tier: Hard Gate = HIGH, Purpose-Gate = MEDIUM, Quality Locks = LOW.

## Findings

### HIGH

1. **Double hyphens used as dashes** (R-02). Seven messages use ` -- ` the way an em dash would be used.
   - "No barangay data yet -- this fills in once citizens have a barangay on file." → "No barangay data yet. It fills in once citizens have a barangay on file."
   - "Built from the Visualizations page -- consolidated data from any office." → "Built from the Visualizations page, with data from any office."
   - "Nothing on the dashboard yet -- use 'Add to dashboard' on the Visualizations page." → "Nothing on the dashboard yet. Use 'Add to dashboard' on the Visualizations page."
   - "Failed to delete office -- it may still have modules or staff accounts" → "Could not delete the office. It may still have programs or staff accounts."
   - "Failed to remove module -- it may already have saved records" → "Could not remove the program. It may already have saved records."
   - "Every module form also collects last/first/middle name, birthdate, and address -- these are the extra..." → "Every program's form already asks for name, birthdate and address. Add only the extra details here."
   - "No offices yet -- create one first" (twice) → "No offices yet. Create one first."

2. **A compliance claim with nothing behind it** (R-36, C-5). The sign-in page says "Internal use only · RA 10173 (Data Privacy Act) compliant". Calling the system compliant is a claim nobody has certified. → "Internal use only · Personal data here is covered by the Data Privacy Act (RA 10173)."

3. **Shouted capitals** (R-36). The rule form reads "matches the conditions but is NOT in the program" and "IS still in the program". → "Not yet served: meets the conditions and is not in the program" / "Needs cleanup: meets the conditions and is still in the program".

### MEDIUM

4. **Database and code words shown to staff** (R-02: text must read as written by a person for this reader).
   - Intake result card: "a new Citizen row was created and linked", "no duplicate Citizen row was created", "recorded with no Citizen row linked" → "added to the City Citizen Registry", "no duplicate was added to the registry", "saved without a registry link".
   - Matching settings: "Controls how aggressively resolvePerson() treats two intake records as the same citizen." (a code function name) → "Controls how close two registrations must be before they are treated as the same citizen."
   - "Module" where the rest of the app says "program": the office delete question ("This office has N module(s)"), "The module name is the office's permanent identifier", "This module has nothing to fill in yet", "No extra details needed for this module", "Failed to load this module's form/fields" → "program". The Create office field labelled "Module name" is actually the office's identifier → "Identifier (cannot be changed later)".
   - "Intakes" (Help) → "Registrations". "Registrations & dedup merges, last 24 months" and the legend "Dedup merges" → "New registrations and merged duplicates, last 24 months" / "Merged duplicates".

5. **Help is out of date and misses most of the new pages** (C-2: everything shown must be true).
   - Admin help still describes the old Overview: "Entries per month for every office, the share of the workload, and the busiest barangays." The share-of-workload chart was removed and the Overview now opens with barangay coverage. → "Which barangays the city's programs reach, entries per month by office, and citizens in more than one program. Click a barangay to filter the page."
   - No help for Program gaps, Disaster lists, Business renewals, Privacy, Import or Link to registry. → one line each, describing what the page shows and who can see it (wording to be drawn only from what the pages already do).
   - The intake result card points to "the Duplicate Review" (the old page name) → "Duplicate review".

### LOW

6. **"module(s)"** in the office delete question: a plural in brackets. → "This office has 1 program" / "N programs".
7. **"Tell your office's story with a chart"** on the office Home: a stock phrase. → "Build a chart of your office's records, for example registrations per month or beneficiaries by barangay."
8. **"admin" and "administrator" both used** for the same person ("An admin will resolve this", "ask an administrator"). → "administrator" everywhere.

## What already passes

- No em dashes, buzzwords (seamless, empower, unlock), exclamation marks, chatbot phrases, fake-candid openers or invented numbers.
- Error and empty-state messages say what happened and what to do next ("The registry search failed, so it's not known whether this person is already registered. Try again before registering a new person.").
- Privacy and disaster-list warnings name the law and the allowed use plainly.
- Most actions name who acts ("Each link is recorded in the audit log. The name the office recorded is kept on the record.").
